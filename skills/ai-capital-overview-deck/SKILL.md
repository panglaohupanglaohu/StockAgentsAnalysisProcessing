# AI Capital Overview Deck

Turn a completed information run into a 20-card research deck. Use the data-card grammar in [references/card-system.md](references/card-system.md).

## Workflow

1. Keep every card traceable to the current run, source URLs, sample size, cutoff, and explicit proxy labels.
2. Prefer charts that match the available data. Never invent valuation, capex, revenue, geography, or company facts; render unavailable measures as a labeled research gap.
3. Generate the complete card set: cover, editorial thesis, method, landscape, evidence volume, comparative signal view, confidence/novelty bubble, source mix, top-three comparison, source ranking, dual-track mix, long tail, focal signal, flow/Sankey, Pareto, evidence hub, uncertainty, adoption-cost proxy, four-risk quadrant, and six takeaways.
4. Use a high-contrast data-journalism visual language: controlled dark surface, restrained accent colors, strong numeric hierarchy, labels, legends, and accessible text alternatives.
5. Publish only after evidence, uncertainty, and citation gates pass. Mark all business and market implications as research/simulation, not investment advice.

## Role handoff

- Lead: choose the editorial thesis and card order.
- Scout / Web Collector: source landscape, timestamps, source metadata.
- Checker / Data Engineer: deduplicate and certify chart inputs.
- Market Mapper / Data Analyst: comparative views, paths, proxy labels, risk matrix.
- Editor / Red Team: narrative, counterevidence, open questions.
- Publisher / Presenter: render the full card deck with responsive and accessible HTML.
